    Talk to Karwa ↗
    Is a booking request a confirmed reservation?

    No. Karwa checks availability and confirms the final price with you on WhatsApp. Your reservation is confirmed only after Karwa gets back to you.

    What rides can I request?

    You can request self-drive cars, car rentals, wedding car hire, outstation trips, bikes, and scooties. Availability is confirmed after you send a request.

    What are the bike and scooty rates?

    Bike rentals are listed at ₹700 for 12 hours. Scooty rentals are listed at ₹600 for 12 hours.

    How do I see my booking status?

    Log in with the account used for your request, then open My Account to view your bookings and their status.

    Where can I read the booking and service terms?

    Read the Booking Terms. Self-drive terms appear in the booking form when you select that service and must be accepted before you send the request.
